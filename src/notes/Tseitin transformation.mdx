<Equation tex={String.raw`
\phi := ((x_1 \lor x_2) \Leftrightarrow x_3) \Rightarrow (\lnot x_4).
`} />

**Step 1.** Associate new variables with the subexpressions.

<div className="tseitin-definitions">
<Equation definition={1} tex={String.raw`y_1 \Leftrightarrow (x_1 \lor x_2)`} />
<Equation definition={2} tex={String.raw`y_2 \Leftrightarrow (y_1 \Leftrightarrow x_3)`} />
<Equation definition={3} tex={String.raw`y_3 \Leftrightarrow \lnot x_4`} />
<Equation definition={4} tex={String.raw`y_4 \Leftrightarrow (y_2 \Rightarrow y_3).`} />
</div>

**Step 2.** Restate the formula using these definitions.

<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
C := {} & y_4 \\
&\land (y_4 \Leftrightarrow (y_2 \Rightarrow y_3)) \\
&\land (y_3 \Leftrightarrow \lnot x_4) \\
&\land (y_2 \Leftrightarrow (y_1 \Leftrightarrow x_3)) \\
&\land (y_1 \Leftrightarrow (x_1 \lor x_2)).
\end{aligned}
`} />

**Step 3.** Convert each definition to CNF.

<div className="tseitin-rule">
<Equation tex={String.raw`a \Leftrightarrow \lnot b`} />
<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
&\equiv (a \lor b) \\
&\land (\lnot a \lor \lnot b)
\end{aligned}
`} />
</div>

<div className="tseitin-rule">
<Equation tex={String.raw`a \Leftrightarrow (b \lor c)`} />
<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
&\equiv (a \lor \lnot b) \\
&\land (a \lor \lnot c) \\
&\land (\lnot a \lor b \lor c)
\end{aligned}
`} />
</div>

<div className="tseitin-rule">
<Equation tex={String.raw`a \Leftrightarrow (b \land c)`} />
<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
&\equiv (\lnot a \lor b) \\
&\land (\lnot a \lor c) \\
&\land (a \lor \lnot b \lor \lnot c)
\end{aligned}
`} />
</div>

<div className="tseitin-rule">
<Equation tex={String.raw`a \Leftrightarrow (b \Rightarrow c)`} />
<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
&\equiv (a \lor b) \\
&\land (a \lor \lnot c) \\
&\land (\lnot a \lor \lnot b \lor c)
\end{aligned}
`} />
</div>

<div className="tseitin-rule">
<Equation tex={String.raw`a \Leftrightarrow (b \Leftrightarrow c)`} />
<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
&\equiv (\lnot a \lor \lnot b \lor c) \\
&\land (\lnot a \lor b \lor \lnot c) \\
&\land (a \lor \lnot b \lor \lnot c) \\
&\land (a \lor b \lor c)
\end{aligned}
`} />
</div>

**CNF:**

<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
C := {} & y_4 \\
&\land (y_4 \lor y_2) \\
&\land (y_4 \lor \lnot y_3) \\
&\land (\lnot y_4 \lor \lnot y_2 \lor y_3) \\[0.5em]
&\land (y_3 \lor x_4) \\
&\land (\lnot y_3 \lor \lnot x_4) \\[0.5em]
&\land (\lnot y_2 \lor \lnot y_1 \lor x_3) \\
&\land (\lnot y_2 \lor y_1 \lor \lnot x_3) \\
&\land (y_2 \lor \lnot y_1 \lor \lnot x_3) \\
&\land (y_2 \lor y_1 \lor x_3) \\[0.5em]
&\land (y_1 \lor \lnot x_1) \\
&\land (y_1 \lor \lnot x_2) \\
&\land (\lnot y_1 \lor x_1 \lor x_2).
\end{aligned}
`} />
