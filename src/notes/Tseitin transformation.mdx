**Step 2.** Restate the formula using these definitions.

<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
C := {} & y_4 \land (y_4 \Leftrightarrow (y_2 \Rightarrow y_3)) \\
&\land (y_3 \Leftrightarrow \lnot x_4) \\
&\land (y_2 \Leftrightarrow (y_1 \Leftrightarrow x_3)) \\
&\land (y_1 \Leftrightarrow (x_1 \lor x_2)).
\end{aligned}

**Step 3.** Convert each definition to CNF.

<Equation tex={String.raw`
\begin{aligned}
a \Leftrightarrow \lnot b &\equiv (a \lor b) \land (\lnot a \lor \lnot b) \\
a \Leftrightarrow (b \lor c) &\equiv (a \lor \lnot b) \land (a \lor \lnot c) \land (\lnot a \lor b \lor c) \\
a \Leftrightarrow (b \land c) &\equiv (\lnot a \lor b) \land (\lnot a \lor c) \land (a \lor \lnot b \lor \lnot c) \\
a \Leftrightarrow (b \Rightarrow c) &\equiv (a \lor b) \land (a \lor \lnot c) \land (\lnot a \lor \lnot b \lor c) \\
a \Leftrightarrow (b \Leftrightarrow c) &\equiv (\lnot a \lor \lnot b \lor c) \land (\lnot a \lor b \lor \lnot c) \land (a \lor \lnot b \lor \lnot c) \land (a \lor b \lor c)
\end{aligned}
`} />
